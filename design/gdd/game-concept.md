## Elevator Pitch

> A web-based educational RPG where you duel an AI opponent by answering English
> questions — every correct answer is a sword strike, every grammar rule a
> shield. Words you miss become cursed runes that you purify through
> spaced-repetition flashcards to enchant your gear.

**Test**: "You battle a ghost opponent in 4 rounds of English questions.
Answer fast to hit harder, and every word you get wrong becomes cursed loot
you must study clean to upgrade your equipment."

## Core Fantasy

You are a wordsmith-warrior: **every vocabulary word is a blade, every grammar
structure a shield**. Reading speed is your attack speed. The arena rewards
fluency under pressure — hesitation is damage taken. And when a word defeats
you, it does not disappear: it follows you as a cursed rune until you master
it, at which point it becomes the very ore you forge your equipment from.

The fantasy is *mastery made visible*: your growing skill is worn on your
character, not recorded in a progress bar.

---

## Unique Hook

**"It's like QuizDuel, AND ALSO your mistakes become crafting material."**

Each question you answer wrong or too slowly drops a *Cổ Ngữ Bị Nguyền*
(cursed rune) — that exact word. The Forge converts a set of three cursed
runes into an Upgrade Stone **via an SRS flashcard quiz you must pass**: answer
all three recall cards correctly to purify them and enchant a piece of
equipment +1. Fail a card and the runes stay cursed.

This inverts the punishment loop of educational games: failure produces the
fuel of progression, and the review queue is disguised as an RPG crafting
station. The hook affects gameplay directly — it is not a theme bolted onto a
quiz.

Supporting differentiator: **speed is a damage statistic**. Every answer's
damage scales with remaining time (`speedMult` up to 1.3×), sub-3-second
answers crit, and one class passive rewards exactly that. Fluency, not just
accuracy, is the skill being tested.

## Player Experience Analysis (MDA Framework)

### Target Aesthetics (What the player FEELS)

| Aesthetic | Priority | How We Deliver It |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |
| **Challenge** | 1 | Timer pressure, speed-scaled damage, opponent accuracy tiers, boss floors |
| **Fantasy** | 2 | RPG arena framing, hero classes, gear with language passives, cursed runes |
| **Sensation** | 3 | Hit shakes, slash VFX, floating damage numbers, combo pops, TTS audio |
| **Discovery** | 4 | Four content modules (Daily/TOEIC/IELTS/IT) with vocabulary and grammar previews |
| **Fellowship** | 5 | Friend-room duels (simulated in MVP), league tables; real social play deferred |
| **Expression** | 6 | Class choice + equipment loadout shapes both stats and question-time affordances |
| **Narrative** | N/A | No story mode exists in the prototype |
| **Submission** | N/A | Not a design goal — the game deliberately applies pressure |

### Key Dynamics (Emergent player behaviors)

- Players optimize for **recall speed**, not just correctness — rereading less,
  trusting first instincts (speedMult makes this strictly better).
- Players **want to lose specific words**: hitting an unknown word in battle
  is also loot acquisition for the Forge.
- Players **equip for their weak skill**: taking the hourglass (+1.5 s on
  grammar) or the etymology charm (word-origin hints) because it covers *their*
  gap.
- Players treat the Forge as **their personal weakness list**, checking cursed
  runes before queueing into the next battle.

### Core Mechanics (Systems we build)

1. **4-round language battle** — vocab strike (ATK), grammar shield (DEF),
   listening pierce, ultimate burst; damage/shield formulas scale off hero ATK/DEF
   and answer speed.
2. **Hero classes with language-anchored passives** — Wordsmith (fast-answer
   damage), Grammarian (streak shields), Arcane Speaker (listening pierce
   damage).
3. **Gear with language passives** — 4 slots × 4 rarities; each item's passive
   hooks a real language affordance (etymology hints, extra grammar time, 50/50
   lifeline, first-letter spell assist).
4. **The Forge (SRS loop)** — cursed runes from battles → 3-card recall quiz
   (2× EN→VI, 1× VI→EN) → Upgrade Stone → equipment +1 level.
5. **Tower PvE** — four content modules as tower floors (3 stages + boss),
   sequential unlocks, pre-battle vocabulary/grammar preview.
6. **Meta layer** — RP/ranked tiers, weekly league tables (PvP and study
   boards), daily quests, gold, XP levels, streaks.

- [x] **Killers/Competitors** — RP ladder, ghost duels ranked by tier; real PvP later

### Flow State Design

- **Onboarding curve**: Auth (self-rated CEFR A2–C1) → class pick → Home with
  one-button ranked queue; battle rules explained in-lobby in 4 round cards.
- **Difficulty scaling**: opponent accuracy by mode (friend 0.65, ranked 0.70,
  stage 0.55, boss 0.75) and boss HP 600 vs stage 420; content tracks self-select
  by CEFR/module.
- **Feedback clarity**: floating damage numbers, speed multiplier HUD, combo
  badges, per-round battle log, end-of-match cursed-rune list.
- **Recovery from failure**: a loss still pays 40 gold / 60 XP **and** drops
  runes — the failure state feeds the fix-it loop (Forge).

## Core Loop

### Moment-to-Moment (30 seconds)
Read question → recall under countdown (10 s base + hero time stat) → answer
→ watch damage/shield resolve with speed multiplier → next round. Keyboard
shortcuts (1–4) for options and direct typing for spelling keep input friction
near zero.

### Short-Term (5-15 minutes)
One battle = 4 rounds (vocab → grammar → listening → ultimate). Battle ends in
Victory/Defeat modal listing **cursed runes gained** → straight into the Forge
or back to queue. This is the "one more match" unit.

### Session-Level (30-120 minutes)
Daily quests (win PvP battles, forge N runes, reach combo ×3, clear a tower
floor) + tower floor progression + a forge session to clear the rune backlog.

### Long-Term Progression
Hero level (1000 XP each) → gear +levels via forge → tower module completion →
ranked tier climb (Bronze 0 → Silver 1000 → Gold 1300 → Diamond 1600 →
Grandmaster 1900 RP, divisions III–I) → weekly league placement.

### Retention Hooks
- **Curiosity**: locked tower floors and boss fights; undiscovered item passives
- **Investment**: cursed runes waiting in the Forge (a visible debt list);
  gear levels that would decay in value if abandoned
- **Mastery**: speed-scores, max-combo records, league study board (mastered
  words + daily streak)
- **Social**: `INTENT UNKNOWN` for MVP — simulated league peers in prototype

## Game Pillars

### Pillar 1: Speed Is Damage
Fluency under time pressure is the core skill. Every formula must reward
answering faster, never just answering.

*Design test*: If a new question type is proposed with untimed scoring, this
pillar says: add a speed component or make it a non-battle mode (e.g., Forge
cards).

### Pillar 2: Mistakes Are Materials
A wrong answer must always produce something the player can act on — cursed
runes feed the Forge; the Forge feeds gear; gear feeds the next battle.

*Design test*: If a feature adds a failure state, this pillar says: route the
failure into the SRS loop (or explicitly justify why it shouldn't).

### Pillar 3: Knowledge Is Gear
Every equipment passive must teach or scaffold a real language skill
(etymology, collocations, IPA, grammar time pressure). No pure stat sticks.

*Design test*: A proposed sword with "+10 ATK, no passive" fails this pillar —
see `dagger-idiom`, the prototype's deliberate common-tier exception whose
flavor text owns it ("Không có nội tại. Đơn giản là sắc bén.").

### Pillar 4: Honest Ghosts
The single-player AI ("Shadow Ghost") is the product, not a stand-in pretending
to be multiplayer. The UI must never fake presence it doesn't have.

*Design test*: A lobby counter ("1,284 người đang tìm trận") that implies real
players fails this pillar at MVP unless labeled as flavor/simulated — the
prototype already labels ranked as ghost duels in the match banner.
**[CONFIRMED 2026-11-08: single-player + AI is core design]**

### Anti-Pillars (What This Game Is NOT)

- **NOT an action game**: no reflex mechanics — the only "action" is recall speed.
- **NOT a grind-wall**: progression must come from practicing weak words, never
  from replaying mastered content (Forge cards draw from *your* cursed runes).
- **NOT fake-live**: no fabricated online counts or invented opponents presented
  as real players.
- **NOT an LMS**: no lesson trees, video courses, or homework flows — learning
  happens inside the battle/forge loop.

## Risks and Open Questions

### Design Risks
- **Fixed 4-question battles**: the prototype replays the same 4 questions per
  set on every battle (tower stages included — stage number changes the opponent,
  not the questions). Without a question-pool/mix system, the core loop burns out
  fast. *Highest-priority design gap.*
- **Ultimate round is currently guaranteed**: prototype auto-sets mana to 100
  when the ultimate round begins, making rounds 1–3 mana-building cosmetic.
  Authored intent is earned mana **[CONFIRMED 2026-11-08]** — production must
  gate the ult on mana actually accumulated (≈1 correct answer per prior round
  at +30 each).
- **Opponent AI is a dice roll**: `correct = random() < accuracy` with no
  adaptive difficulty or personality; fine for MVP, weak for long-term mastery
  feel.
- **Forge quiz has no real spacing** — it reviews immediately, same-session.
  The SRS claim needs actual scheduling (or renaming) to honor Pillar 2 long-term.

### Technical Risks
- **Backend scope** (accounts, progress, content serving) is new ground — the
  prototype has none of it **[CONFIRMED 2026-11-08: backend is required]**.
- **Web Speech API variance**: voice quality/availability differs per browser/OS;
  listening rounds degrade unfairly without a fallback (e.g., IPA-first mode).
- **Mic scoring is simulated** (random 88–98%) **[CONFIRMED 2026-11-08: keep
  simulated for MVP]** — must be wrapped behind an interface so a real scoring
  engine can drop in without gameplay changes.

### Market Risks
- Crowded space (Duolingo et al.); differentiation rests on the duel fantasy +
  forge loop, which must survive contact with real learners. `INTENT UNKNOWN`:
  positioning vs. existing Vietnamese-market apps.
- Monetization undefined — F2P-with-cosmetics vs. premium vs. exam-prep pricing
  all imply different product constraints.

### Scope Risks
- Content volume (question banks per CEFR band × module) is the long pole; the
  prototype's 20 questions are a template, not a library.
- Real PvP, if greenlit post-MVP, adds netcode/server-authority scope — kept
  out of MVP by the single-player decision.

### Open Questions
1. **Question-pool design**: how many questions per battle set minimum, and how
   are they mixed per battle? (Resolve in `/map-systems` → battle-system GDD)
2. **Persistence boundary**: what lives server-side (progress? content? both?)
   and what may stay client-cached? (ADR)
3. **Guest mode**: prototype supports guest entry with unsaved progress — keep
   for MVP? `INTENT UNKNOWN`
4. **Monetization & league realism**: deferred; revisit before Production.

## Prototype Analysis (reverse-documentation findings)

**Prototype**: React 19 + Vite 8 + Tailwind v4 SPA, ~4,200 lines across 14
source files, built in Figma Make. State via a single React context
(`src/game/store.tsx`); all rules as plain data + pure functions
(`src/game/data.ts`); battle logic centralized in `src/screens/Battle.tsx`.

**Outcome**: ✅ **Validated** — the concept is fully playable end-to-end:
auth → class pick → battles → forge → tower → leagues, with the SRS hook
wired to real battle outcomes.

- ✅ **Validated**: speed-damage feel (timer + multiplier + crits are legible
  in one battle); forge loop is compelling (losses convert to visible goals);
  class passives map cleanly to language skills; 4-round structure gives each
  question skill a distinct combat identity.
- ⚠️ **Needs Work**: question variety per set (4 fixed questions); mana economy
  (auto-filled ult); forge spacing; opponent AI depth; persistence (none).
- ❌ **Invalidated**: nothing fundamental — no mechanic was tried and abandoned
  in the prototype.

**Key learnings carried forward**:
- The RPG frame does heavy lifting: damage numbers, shields and crits make
  *recall speed* legible — the same quiz with a progress bar would not.
- Centralizing all tuning in `data.ts` (classes, items, formulas' constants,
  question sets) kept the prototype tweakable; production should keep this
  (data-driven values are also a studio coding standard).
- UI polish (hit shake, floating numbers, combo pops) is load-bearing for the
  power fantasy — budget for it as first-class work, not garnish.

**Reusable code**: the prototype is a high-fidelity reference implementation.
Realistic reuse as *reference*, not as production base (production needs
backend integration, auth, content pipeline, tests):
- `src/game/data.ts` — types, stat math, content schemas: **high** reuse value
- `src/game/store.tsx` — state shape and reducers: **medium** (will be
  re-plumbed to a server-backed store)
- `src/screens/battle/RoundPanels.tsx` — question interactions incl. spell
  tiles + keyboard input: **high**
- Battle timing/resolve sequencing (setTimeout choreography): **medium** —
  needs a deterministic tick model for tests
- Auth screen: **low** (simulated only)

## MVP Definition

**Core hypothesis**: *Speed-scored language battles + the forge-SRS loop are
fun enough to drive return sessions — without any real opponents.*

**Required for MVP**:
1. 4-round battle vs ghost AI with speed-scaled damage/shield/crit formulas
2. Earned-mana ultimate round (mic or spell completion) **[per 2026-11-08
   decision — differs from prototype]**
3. Forge SRS loop converting missed words into equipment upgrades
4. One hero class set (3 classes) + equip/passive system
5. Tower PvE on one module (then all four), with per-stage question pools
6. Real backend: accounts + server-side progress **[CONFIRMED 2026-11-08]**
7. Persistent rune queue across sessions (backend-backed)

**Explicitly NOT in MVP** (deferred):
- Real-time PvP with humans
- Real pronunciation scoring engine (simulated behind an interface)
- Weekly leagues with real players (simulated boards acceptable)
- Monetization of any kind
- Streak-freeze/shop systems beyond the prototype's quest set
